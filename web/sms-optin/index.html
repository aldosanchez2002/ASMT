aslog text message consent

Last updated October 30, 2026

aslog is a program operated by Aldo Sanchez. Only employees of the fleet use the aslog receipts number, (915) 529-1018, to send photos of parts receipts. Employees opt in verbally when the fleet office adds them to the program.

Script the fleet office reads

"aslog, run by Aldo Sanchez, will text you from (915) 529-1018 to confirm the parts receipts you send and ask which truck they are for. Message frequency varies with how many receipts you send, usually 1-2 replies per receipt. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help and STOP to opt out at any time. Agreeing is not a condition of employment; you can also upload receipts at aslog.dev. Our Terms & Conditions are at aslog.dev/terms and our Privacy Policy at aslog.dev/privacy. Do you agree to receive these texts?"

The employee must answer yes. Only employees who say yes are added to the approved list; texts from any other number are never answered.

Confirmation message

Sent with the employee's first text to the number:

"aslog: You're enrolled to send parts receipts to this number. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."
