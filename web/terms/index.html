aslog is a program operated by Aldo Sanchez. aslog.dev is the maintenance system for a trucking fleet, and the aslog receipts number, (915) 529-1018, is for the fleet's employees to send photos of parts receipts.

SMS Terms

  • Program name: aslog parts receipts, operated by Aldo Sanchez.
  • Program description: employees text photos of parts receipts to (915) 529-1018. aslog replies to confirm the receipt was saved, to say what was read from it, or to ask which truck it is for. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.
  • How you join: the fleet office asks each employee, when they are added to the program, whether they agree to receive these texts, and only employees who say yes are added. Texts from any other number are never answered. Your first text gets a confirmation message. Agreeing to texts is not a condition of employment; receipts can also be uploaded at aslog.dev.
  • Message frequency: message frequency varies with how many receipts you send, usually one or two replies per receipt.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply. (Msg & data rates may apply.)
  • Opt out: text STOP to (915) 529-1018 at any time to cancel. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed, and no more messages after that. Text START to join again.
  • Help: text HELP to (915) 529-1018 for help.
  • Support contact: text HELP to (915) 529-1018, or ask the fleet office.
