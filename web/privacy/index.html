Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

aslog.dev is the fleet maintenance system for our company's trucks and trailers. This page explains what we collect through it, including text messages sent to our receipts number, (915) 529-1018.

Who uses it

Only our own employees use aslog.dev and the receipts number. We do not offer it to the public.

What we collect

  • Photos of parts receipts that employees text or upload, and what is read from them (store, items, prices, dates, the truck they are for).
  • The phone number a receipt came from and when it was sent, so we know who sent it.
  • Maintenance records our mechanics enter, and vehicle data (location, odometer) from our fleet tracking provider.

How we use it

Only to run our fleet: recording parts and repairs, tracking costs per truck, and scheduling maintenance. Text messages from this number are replies to receipts an employee sent: a confirmation, what was read, or a question about which truck it is for. We never send marketing messages.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information and text message consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information is processed only by the service providers that run the system (text messaging, data storage, and receipt reading), and only to provide it.

Text messages

Message frequency varies with how many receipts you send. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop messages and HELP for help.

Questions
