Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Who we are

aslog is a program operated by Aldo Sanchez. aslog.dev is the maintenance system for a trucking fleet, used by the fleet's employees (mechanics, parts buyers and office staff). The aslog receipts number is (915) 529-1018. This Privacy Policy explains what aslog collects, including through text messages (SMS/MMS) sent to and from that number, and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Photos of parts receipts that employees text to (915) 529-1018 or upload, and what is read from them (store, items, prices, dates, and the truck they are for).
  • The mobile phone number a text came from, the employee's name, and when it was sent, so we know who sent each receipt.
  • Maintenance records employees enter, and vehicle data (location, odometer) from the fleet's GPS tracking provider.

How we use it

Only to run the fleet: recording parts and repairs, tracking costs per truck, and scheduling maintenance. Text messages from (915) 529-1018 are replies to receipts an employee sent: a confirmation, what was read from the receipt, or a question about which truck it is for. We never send marketing or promotional messages.

SMS opt-in data and mobile information

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Service providers

aslog uses service providers only to run the system: text message delivery, data storage, and reading receipt photos. They process information only to provide those services to aslog and may not use it for their own purposes or for marketing. This never includes sharing text messaging opt-in data or consent.

Opting out of text messages

Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to (915) 529-1018 at any time to stop receiving messages; you will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed. Reply HELP for help.

Keeping and protecting information

Information is kept while it is needed for the fleet's maintenance and cost records, and access is limited to the fleet's own staff.
